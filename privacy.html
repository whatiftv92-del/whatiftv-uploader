Privacy Policy for WhatIfTV Uploader

Last updated: November 20, 2026

WhatIfTV Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool used by the owner of the WhatIfTV YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and schedule their publish times. This policy explains what data the App uses.

1. Data the App accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the App requests permission to:

  • Upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel (scope: youtube.upload).
  • Set video thumbnails and manage video details on that same channel (scope: youtube.force-ssl).

The App does not read viewer data, comments, subscriber lists, analytics, or any data about other channels.

2. How data is stored

The App stores an access token on the owner's own computer so it can upload without signing in each time. This token is not shared with anyone. The App keeps a local log of uploads (video title, YouTube video ID, privacy status, and publish time) on the same computer.

3. Data sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Uploaded videos are sent directly from the owner's computer to YouTube, and are subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Use of Google user data

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data retention and deletion

The access token and upload log remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. To revoke the App's access at any time, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove "WhatIfTV Uploader".
